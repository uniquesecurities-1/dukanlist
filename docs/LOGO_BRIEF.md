## 2. WHAT THE LOGO MUST CONVEY

✅ **Local + Bharat** — feel of small-town India + tier-2/3 city pride
✅ **Verified trust** — customer can rely on listings
✅ **Inclusivity** — not just shops; doctors, clinics, schools, offices, showrooms all welcome
✅ **Modernity** — competes with global apps (Google, Justdial), looks 2026+
✅ **Discovery** — finding something, location-aware
✅ **Community** — neighborhood feel, not corporate cold

❌ **AVOID:**
- Generic "shopping bag" iconography (excludes doctors/professionals)
- Western corporate sterility (Google-Lite look)
- Overly traditional/festive ornaments (feels dated)
- Stock "house with location pin" (overused)
- Pure text-only mark (needs an icon for app stores)

### 🅰️ DIRECTION A — "Location Pin That's Also a Door"
**Concept:** A modern teardrop location pin where the bottom point is shaped like an open doorway/shopfront, suggesting "find the local entry point to any business."

**Canva Prompt:**

## 10. LOGO RULES — DO's & DON'Ts

### ✅ DO
- Always use approved saffron gradient
- Maintain padding of at least 25% of logo height around it
- Use vector format whenever possible
- Test at 16×16 (favicon size) — must remain recognizable
- Use against cream, white, charcoal backgrounds
- Pair with Manrope or Noto Sans Devanagari typefaces only

### ❌ DON'T
- Change colors arbitrarily
- Add drop shadows or gradients to the wordmark
- Stretch or distort the logo
- Place on busy photo backgrounds without scrim
- Use generic shopping cart/bag icons
- Add ".com" or "App" to the brand name
- Use the logo alongside another brand name (Unique Securities, etc. — strictly separate)

## 12. QUICK-START IN CANVA

1. Open **canva.com** → log in
2. Click **"Create a design"** → **"Logo"** template (500×500)
3. In the search bar, paste the **Super Prompt** from Section 7
4. Browse generated options, pick closest to your liking
5. Manually adjust:
   - Replace fonts with **Manrope** (Canva has it)
   - Apply **saffron gradient** (#FF6B1A → #E55100) to mark
   - Add Devanagari **द** element if going Direction F (Canva has Devanagari fonts)
6. Click **Download** → choose **SVG** (or PNG transparent if SVG locked)
7. Export all sizes from Section 8

**Pro tip:** Canva Pro lets you download SVG and Background Remover. Worth ₹500/month if you're designing many assets.
