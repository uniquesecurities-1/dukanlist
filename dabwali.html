About Mandi Dabwali

Mandi Dabwali is a vibrant municipality in Sirsa district, Haryana, sitting right at the border with Punjab and Rajasthan. Known as a major agricultural trading hub for cotton, mustard and wheat, the town has a tight-knit business community where personal trust drives every transaction.

DukanList was built right here — Chautala Road, near IndusInd Bank — to bring every business online with zero cost, zero technical hassle. From your pharmacist to your insurance advisor, every local business deserves to be discovered by neighbours and travellers alike.
