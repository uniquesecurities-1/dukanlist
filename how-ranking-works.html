Why recent reviews count more

A shop that delighted customers two years ago and has been silent since is not the same as one delighting them this month. A directory that ignores the difference sends people to the wrong door. So a fresh review is worth more than an old one — which also means the top spots have to be re-earned, not won once and kept forever.

Why answering reviews earns points

Replying in public is the clearest signal a shopkeeper can give that he stands behind his work — a customer reading it learns more about the shop than any badge could tell them. It costs nothing but a minute, and it is the fastest way up the list for most shops.

What we do not do

No paid ranking. There is no amount of money that moves a shop up. We do not sell positions, and we never will.

No hidden thumb on the scale. The number you see on the Top page, the rank in your own panel, and the one printed on a certificate all come from the same calculation. If they ever disagree, that is a bug — tell us.

Fake reviews

Reviews are tied to a phone number, one per shop. Anyone can flag a review that looks fake, and flagged reviews come to us for a human to look at. A shop caught arranging fake reviews is removed from the rankings — the list is only worth anything if the people reading it can trust it.
