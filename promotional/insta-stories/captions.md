## ☞ Posting strategy (suggested order):

1. **Pehli baar** — saari 6 slides ek saath post karein (1 hour ka gap mein, ya seedha back-to-back).
2. **Har 5-7 din baad** — sirf 1 slide repost karein randomly. Jaise:
   - Monday: Slide 3 (QR Poster)
   - Friday: Slide 5 (Spotlight)
   - Sunday: Slide 6 (CTA)
3. **Important** — har post mein **link sticker** se dukanlist.com daalein, taaki click count ho.
