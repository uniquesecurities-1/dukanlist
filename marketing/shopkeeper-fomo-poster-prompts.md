## 🎯 HOW TO USE

1. **Copy the AI Image Prompt** (the code block)
2. Paste into:
   - **Midjourney** / **Discord bot** → prefix with `/imagine `
   - **Ideogram.ai** (best for Hindi text) → free tier available
   - **Kling AI** → for video variants
   - **DALL-E 3** (via ChatGPT Plus)
   - **Google Gemini Imagen** → free
3. Generate 3-4 variations, pick best
4. Open in Canva → add the **Overlay Text** on top (use Manrope + Noto Sans Devanagari fonts)
5. Export as PNG (1080x1920)
6. Post on WhatsApp Status / Instagram Story / Facebook / share as poster
