## 🎯 THE MAIN CUSTOMIZATION PROMPT

### Copy-paste this into NotebookLM's "Customize" field:

```
Create a compelling, story-driven promotional video for DukanList — a free 
local business directory app that just launched on Google Play. The video must 
cover the COMPLETE user journey from shopkeepers to end customers.

STRUCTURE (follow this order strictly):

Part 1 — THE PROBLEM (60-90 seconds)
Open with the real pain: In tier-2 and tier-3 Indian towns like Mandi 
Dabwali, Sirsa, Bathinda, Mansa, and Muktsar, local businesses are almost 
invisible online. The trusted mechanic, the reliable pharmacy owner, the 
family dentist who has served for 30 years — customers can't find them on 
Google Maps. Google Maps shows outdated or missing information. Justdial 
charges high fees. Facebook shops aren't discoverable by neighbors. Meanwhile 
customers waste hours asking WhatsApp groups "kisi ko achha electrician pata 
hai?" This gap is exactly what DukanList solves.

Part 2 — INTRODUCING DUKANLIST (60 seconds)
DukanList is Bharat's local business directory — 100% free for shop owners, 
100% free for customers, built specifically for small-town India. Launched 
from Mandi Dabwali and expanding across Haryana, Punjab, and Rajasthan. Now 
LIVE on Google Play as a lightweight 1.8 MB Android app. Introduce the app 
with confidence: verified listings, no ads, no data selling, no hidden costs.

Part 3 — THE SHOPKEEPER'S JOURNEY (3-4 minutes) — MOST IMPORTANT SECTION
Walk through EVERY step a shopkeeper takes:

1. Discovery: How a shopkeeper finds DukanList (WhatsApp forward from a 
   neighbor, banner in Mandi Dabwali market, or referral from another shop).

2. Registration in 5 minutes:
   - Open the app or dukanlist.com
   - Tap "+ Register Free"
   - Enter mobile number
   - Receive OTP for verification
   - Add shop name, category (choose from 370+), city, address
   - Upload 1-5 shop photos (a good storefront photo triples clicks)
   - Add opening hours, services offered, USP (unique selling proposition)
   - Optional: WhatsApp number for direct chat
   - Submit — verification usually complete within 24 hours

3. What happens next:
   - Verified badge appears on their listing
   - Shop becomes discoverable across all of DukanList's growing user base
   - Every time a customer searches their category in their city, they appear
   - Real-time notifications when customers view, call, or WhatsApp

4. Lead management:
   - Owner Panel dashboard shows lead count per day
   - See how many people saw your listing, called you, WhatsApp'd you
   - Reply to reviews to build trust
   - Add photos anytime to stand out (3x more clicks with 5+ photos)
   - Enable "Open Now" indicator so customers know real-time availability

5. Growth opportunities:
   - Golden Pages listing for extra visibility
   - Featured Shop of the Week program (curated weekly)
   - Top 10 rankings by category and city (auto-updated monthly)
   - Referral program: refer 5 shop owners, both get 6 months featured FREE
   - Public Q&A on shop pages builds trust and SEO
   - WhatsApp Poster Studio: 16 festive themes, 3 sizes, free HD PNG for 
     shop's WhatsApp status marketing

6. Real example: mention "Dr Sachdeva's Dinesh Dental Clinic in Mandi 
   Dabwali is a verified listing on DukanList." Also mention "Ajay 
   Handloom" (textile shop) and "Rk Photography" (photography studio) as 
   trusted local names using 
   DukanList to grow.

Part 4 — THE CUSTOMER'S JOURNEY (2-3 minutes)
Walk through the customer experience:

1. Discovery: Customer searches something on Google, ends up at 
   dukanlist.com/search. Or gets a WhatsApp forward with a shop's DukanList 
   link. Or installs the app from Play Store after seeing a poster in the 
   market.

2. Finding a business — 3 taps:
   - Open app
   - Type category (e.g., "electrician") or tap Quick Pick chip
   - Select city (Mandi Dabwali, Sirsa, Bathinda, or others)
   - See list of verified shops with photos, ratings, distance, and 
     "Open Now" indicators
   - Tap any shop for full details

3. Contact in one tap:
   - Big "Call" button — direct phone call to shop owner
   - Big "WhatsApp" button — opens WhatsApp chat with the shop
   - "Get Directions" button — opens Google Maps with the exact address
   - Read genuine customer reviews before contacting

4. Community feature — Pucho Bhai:
   - Ask your neighborhood anything: "Which pharmacy is open at midnight?", 
     "Best CA in Bathinda for GST filing?", "Recommend a good tailor near 
     Chotala Road?"
   - Locals reply with real answers
   - Best answers rise to the top with community upvotes
   - No login needed to ask or answer
   - Tag any DukanList shop in your reply for direct contact

5. Additional customer benefits:
   - Works offline for previously viewed shops
   - Save favorite shops to personal shortlist
   - Get notified of new deals from your favorite shops
   - "Open Now Only" filter for immediate needs
   - Multilingual: English and Hindi throughout

Part 5 — WHY DUKANLIST IS DIFFERENT (60-90 seconds)
Emphasize what makes DukanList uniquely valuable:
- NO ads anywhere in the app (unlike Justdial, Sulekha)
- NO data selling to third parties
- NO SMS spam or marketing emails
- NO "featured listing" payment ladders (small shops don't get buried)
- NO commission on any transactions
- Verified via mobile OTP + pincode-address match + real shop photo
- Anti-fake system detects duplicate or suspicious listings
- Auto-updated freshness — inactive listings get flagged
- Built mobile-first for low-data networks and entry-level Android phones
- Full Hindi support with proper Noto Sans Devanagari fonts
- Made in Bharat, for Bharat — designed for tier-2, tier-3 cities not just 
  metros
- Lightweight: 1.8 MB app download, works smoothly on 2 GB RAM phones

Part 6 — TRUST AND CREDIBILITY (45-60 seconds)
DukanList is built by a team that has been serving customers in Mandi 
Dabwali since 2009 through their financial services practice, with a proven 
track record of building trusted local relationships. The DukanList app is 
their commitment to bringing every local business — big or small — into 
the digital age without middlemen, without exploitation, and without cost. 
The team personally visits shops to help them onboard, offers WhatsApp 
support for any questions, and treats every listing as a partnership 
rather than a transaction.

Part 7 — THE CALL TO ACTION (30-45 seconds)
Close with two clear paths:

FOR SHOPKEEPERS:
"If you own a shop, clinic, office, or any business — no matter how small — 
list your business FREE on DukanList today. Go to dukanlist.com and tap 
Register Free, or install the Android app from Google Play. Takes 5 minutes. 
Your business gets a digital identity that will serve you for years. Every 
customer who finds you through DukanList costs you nothing."

FOR CUSTOMERS:
"If you're tired of asking WhatsApp groups for shop recommendations, install 
DukanList from Google Play. Find any local business in 3 taps. Call, 
WhatsApp, or get directions instantly. It's free, it works offline, and 
it's built for people like you who value real, verified information."

Final tagline: "DukanList — Every Local Name, One Identity. Bharat ki 
local directory, Bharat ke liye."

TONE GUIDANCE:
- Warm, conversational, aspirational
- Speak to the audience as neighbors, not customers
- Use small-town Indian sensibilities — practical, relationship-driven, 
  trust-focused
- Avoid corporate jargon or Silicon Valley tech-speak
- Include specific place names and shop names for authenticity
- Balance rational benefits (free, fast, verified) with emotional benefits 
  (support local, build trust, help neighbors)
- Use "your neighbor", "your shop", "your customer" — inclusive language
- Speak WITH the audience, not AT them

CRUCIAL CONSTRAINTS:
1. Do NOT depict Mandi Dabwali or other tier-3 towns as "old", 
   "backward", or "rural" — they are modern commercial hubs with 
   professional businesses. Dabwali specifically is a thriving cotton, 
   grain, and hosiery trade town with wide commercial streets, modern 
   glass-front shops, and active commerce. Treat it with the same respect 
   you would treat Delhi's Connaught Place or Mumbai's Colaba.

2. Feature these REAL business names throughout (they are actual verified 
   listings in the DukanList database, not fictional):
   - Dr Sachdeva's Dinesh Dental Clinic & Implant Centre (Dentist, 
     Mandi Dabwali) — 5.0 stars, 47 reviews
   - Dhaliwal Hospital (Healthcare, Mandi Dabwali)
   - Ajay Handloom (Textiles, Mandi Dabwali) — 5-star reviews
   - Rk Photography (Photography studio, Mandi Dabwali)
   - Sk Chunni Dai Centre (Sweets/Mithai, Mandi Dabwali) — recent addition
   - Goyal Saree (Sarees/Apparel, Mandi Dabwali)

3. NEVER mention specific financial credentials like SEBI registration or 
   AMFI registration in the video. Keep the DukanList narrative separate 
   from any financial services references.

4. Include Hindi phrases naturally when they add authenticity:
   - "पूछो भाई" for the community Q&A feature
   - "हर दुकान, हर डॉक्टर, हर professional"
   - "एक tap में सब कुछ"
   - "अपने मोहल्ले से पूछो"
   Do not translate everything — let bilingualism feel natural.

5. Emphasize the free-forever promise MULTIPLE times. This is the single 
   most important differentiator against Justdial and IndiaMart.

6. The app is available at: play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dukanlist.twa
   The website is: dukanlist.com
   Mention both throughout.

7. Founder credit ONLY as "the DukanList team" or "the team behind 
   DukanList" — do not name specific founders unless the source material 
   provides that context appropriately.

8. Do NOT include forward-looking statistics that aren't in the source. If 
   listing counts, review counts, or user counts are needed, only use 
   numbers actually in the source material (don't fabricate 
   "50,000 users" etc.).
```

## 📝 HOW TO USE THIS IN NOTEBOOKLM

### Step 1: Create a new NotebookLM notebook
Go to notebooklm.google.com → New notebook

### Step 2: Add sources
Upload the .txt files AND paste the URL sources listed above.

### Step 3: Generate Video Overview
- Click the "Video Overview" or "Studio" panel
- Click "Customize" (if available in your version)
- Paste the MAIN CUSTOMIZATION PROMPT above
- Click "Generate"

### Step 4: Wait 5-10 minutes
NotebookLM will:
- Analyze your sources
- Create a script based on your customization
- Generate AI voice narration
- Compose visuals with your content
- Output an MP4 video

### Step 5: Review and iterate
If output isn't perfect:
- Try a shorter variant
- Add more specific source material
- Adjust the tone guidance in the prompt

### Step 6: Distribute
Once you have a great video:
- Upload to YouTube (SEO win for dukanlist.com)
- Share on WhatsApp Status (splits automatically)
- Share as Instagram Reel (edit to under 90 seconds)
- Embed on dukanlist.com/about page
- Send to local newspapers as embedded content
