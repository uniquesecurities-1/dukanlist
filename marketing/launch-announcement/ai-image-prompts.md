## 🔧 Tips for best results

### ChatGPT (DALL-E 3) — easiest

1. Open ChatGPT (paid Plus version recommended for unlimited images)
2. Paste full prompt
3. If output not perfect, say: "Make the phone larger and the shop signage more clearly visible"
4. Iterate 2-3 times for best version
5. Right-click → Save image

### Midjourney — premium quality

Add at end of prompt:
```
--ar 9:16 --style raw --v 6 --quality 2
```

### Sora — for animated versions

Add at start: "Animated 5-second loop:"

### Google Imagen / Vertex AI

Works as-is, very photo-realistic.
