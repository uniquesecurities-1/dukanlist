## 🎵 MUSIC BRIEF (संगीत के लिए निर्देश)

### Style:
**Indian orchestral fusion** — cinematic, warm, aspirational

### Structure (60-second version):

| Time | Section | Instruments |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:10 | Intro | Solo bansuri + soft strings pad |
| 0:10-0:25 | Build (Scene 2-3) | Tabla enters, sarangi joins, energy builds |
| 0:25-0:40 | Peak (Scene 3 climax + Scene 4 montage) | Full orchestra + dholak + choral "aaa" |
| 0:40-0:52 | Resolution (Scene 5 CTA) | Orchestral hit → gentle descend |
| 0:52-0:60 | Sonic logo | Signature 3-note bansuri motif |

### Tempo:
- 90 BPM (moderate, allows dialogue clarity)
- Slow build to 100 BPM by peak

### Key:
- G major (uplifting, hopeful)

### Reference tracks (composer के लिए):
- A.R. Rahman: **"Ye Jo Des Hai Tera"** (Swades) — patriotic warmth
- A.R. Rahman: **"Roobaroo"** (Rang De Basanti) — youth energy
- Salim-Sulaiman: **"Ajj Din Chadheya"** (Love Aaj Kal) — melodic + modern
- Hans Zimmer: **"Time"** (Inception) — cinematic build

### Voice-over voice:
- Male, 35-45 age
- Warm, mature, non-regional Hindi accent
- Reference: Bade B (Big B) minus the drama — softer, more accessible
- OR: Manoj Bajpayee style (understated confidence)

### Where to get music:
- **Best**: Local composer (Delhi/Mumbai) — ₹15,000-30,000
- **Budget**: Suno.ai custom generation — ₹500 for 5 iterations
- **Free**: Epidemic Sound / Artlist (subscription based)

### Voice-over recording:
- **Studio**: Local recording studio in Sirsa/Dabwali — ₹1,500-3,000 per session
- **Home studio**: Deepak Sir के office में USB mic (Blue Yeti) — ₹0 (if own)
- **Voice actor**: Sirsa/Chandigarh voice artists — ₹2,000-5,000 for single ad

## 🎨 FINAL VISUAL & TONAL GUIDELINES

### DO ✅
- Show Dabwali as a **modern commercial town** — clean roads, glass shops, active commerce
- Feature **real shops** with permission (Ajay Handloom, Dr Sachdeva's, Rk Photography)
- Use **warm golden hour light** wherever possible
- Include **bilingual signage** in market shots
- Show **diverse Bharat** — different ages, genders, religions, professions
- Emphasize **free forever** promise (verbal + text)
- End with strong **Google Play CTA**
- Use **saffron color palette** subtly throughout
