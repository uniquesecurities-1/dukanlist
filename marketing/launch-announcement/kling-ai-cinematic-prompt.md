## 🎬 KLING AI SPECIFIC TIPS

### General settings
- **Version**: Kling 1.6 for standard quality, Kling 2.0 for premium
- **Duration**: 5 seconds per clip (Kling's sweet spot)
- **Motion strength**: Medium-High (4-5 out of 5) for cinematic camera moves
- **Creativity**: 0.5-0.7 (balanced — enough interpretation, not chaotic)
- **Aspect ratio**: 9:16 (vertical) for all clips
- **CFG scale**: 7-8 (default is usually fine)

### For faces
Add to every scene prompt with people:
```
Detailed realistic Indian facial features, natural skin texture, 
warm smile, genuine expression, sharp focus on eyes, ARRI cinema 
skin tones
```

### For text on phone
Kling struggles with readable app UI text. **Better approach**:
- Generate the phone shot WITHOUT trying to show real DukanList UI
- In post-production, mask the phone screen area
- Composite your actual app screenshot with slight motion blur
- Adds precision without fighting the AI

### For bilingual signage in shots
Same approach — let Kling generate generic "Hindi + English text" 
signage. Don't specify exact words (AI will misspell). In post-
production, add a "Verified on DukanList" sticker overlay via After 
Effects or DaVinci Fusion.

### Camera movement keywords that work well in Kling
- "slow smooth dolly in"
- "cinematic drone descent"
- "gentle orbit shot"
- "slow push-in"
- "handheld cinematic style"
- "slow tracking shot"

### Lighting keywords that work well
- "magical golden hour"
- "warm saffron backlighting"
- "cinematic rim light"
- "soft window light"
- "magic hour ambient"

### Style/quality keywords
- "shot on ARRI Alexa"
- "35mm anamorphic lens"
- "shallow depth of field"
- "cinematic color grade"
- "photorealistic"
- "Roger Deakins aesthetic"


### Step 3: Text overlays (add in editor, NOT in Kling)

| 0:35-0:40 | Logo + tagline (already in scene) |

### Step 4: Music sync
- Import your generated music
- Sync tabla hit to Scene 8 logo reveal
- Duck music -6dB behind voiceover if any
- Add subtle sound design: shop bell tinkle in scene 2, phone notification sound in scene 3, WhatsApp ping in scene 4

### Step 5: Color grade
Apply LUT: "Saffron Golden Bharat" style
- Lift shadows slightly warm
- Boost saturation on saffron/orange +10%
- Slight teal in shadows for complementary contrast
- Overall exposure warm

### Step 6: Export
- 1080×1920 (9:16 vertical)
- H.264 codec
- 60 Mbps bitrate
- 30 or 60 FPS
- Filename: `DukanList-Launch-Ad-40sec-v1.mp4`
