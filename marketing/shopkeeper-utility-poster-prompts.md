## 🎯 HOW TO USE

1. **Copy the AI Image Prompt** (the code block)
2. Paste into:
   - **Ideogram.ai** (best for Hindi text, free tier)
   - **Midjourney** (best photo-realism)
   - **DALL-E 3** (via ChatGPT Plus)
   - **Google Gemini Imagen** (free)
   - **Kling AI** (for video versions)
3. Generate 3-4 variations, pick the best
4. Open in **Canva** → add the **Overlay Text** on top
   - Fonts: **Manrope Bold** (English) + **Noto Sans Devanagari Bold** (Hindi)
5. Export as PNG (1080x1920)
6. Post on WhatsApp Status / IG Story / Facebook / share as poster
