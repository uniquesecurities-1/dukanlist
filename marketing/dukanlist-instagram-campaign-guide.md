## 1. Campaign Strategy — 3 Phases

| Phase | Days | Goal | Primary Audience | Main CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1: Curiosity** | 1–14 | Make name DukanList recognisable | Everyone in Dabwali — customers + shopkeepers | "Check karo dukanlist.com" |
| **Phase 2: Owner acquisition** | 15–35 | Onboard 200+ business owners | Shopkeepers 25–55 in Dabwali area | "Apni dukaan free me list karo" |
| **Phase 3: Customer adoption** | 36–60 | Drive search traffic | Households 22–50 looking for local services | "Doctor / Plumber chahiye? Pucho Bhai" |

**Why this order matters:** Without owners on the platform, customer searches fail and they bounce. Without customers, owners feel listed in vacuum. Phase 1 plants the seed, Phase 2 builds supply, Phase 3 drives demand.

## 5. Instagram Ads Manager — Step-by-Step

**Prerequisites:**
- Instagram Business or Creator account for `@dukanlist` (or whatever handle)
- Facebook Page linked to Instagram (this is mandatory for Ads Manager)
- Meta Business Suite account with a verified payment method

### Step 5.1 — Create the campaign

1. Open **Meta Ads Manager** at `https://business.facebook.com/adsmanager`
2. Click green **+ Create** button
3. Choose **Buying Type: Auction** and **Campaign Objective**:
   - Phase 1 → **Engagement** or **Awareness**
   - Phase 2 → **Leads** (collects email/mobile inline)
   - Phase 3 → **Traffic** (drives to dukanlist.com)
4. Campaign name: `DL-MandiDabwali-Phase1-Awareness-202607`
   (Naming convention: project-area-phase-objective-yearmonth)
5. **Set Campaign Spending Limit** to ₹3,000 (safety cap for first week)
6. **Advantage+ Campaign Budget:** ON (let Meta optimise across ad sets)

### Step 5.2 — Audience setup (ad set level)

1. **Location:**
   - Drop pin: `29.9491°N, 74.7448°E` (Mandi Dabwali centre)
   - Radius: 10 km initially (Phase 1)
   - Include: "People living in this location"
   - Do NOT include "recently in this location" (tourists pollute data)
2. **Age:** 22–55
3. **Gender:** All
4. **Detailed targeting:**
   - For owners: `Small business owners` + `Local advertising` + `Entrepreneurship (small business)`
   - For customers: `Online shopping` + `Local services` + `Indian cooking`
5. **Languages:** Hindi, Punjabi, English (selecting these tells Meta to show the ad to people reading these languages — doesn't translate the ad)
6. **Placements:** Manual → Tick **Instagram Feed + Instagram Stories + Instagram Reels** only. Untick Facebook initially (most of your Phase 1 audience is on Instagram in this region).
7. **Daily budget:** ₹300/day (we'll explain why in §6)
8. **Schedule:** Continuous (let it run, monitor weekly)

### Step 5.3 — Ad-level setup

1. **Identity:** Choose your Instagram business handle (e.g., `@dukanlist`)
2. **Format:** Match the creative (Single image / Carousel / Reel)
3. **Media:** Upload the design you made (per Creative #1–#5)
4. **Primary text:** Paste body copy
5. **Headline:** Paste your headline
6. **Description:** Optional — use a strong tagline
7. **Destination URL:**
   - Phase 1 → `dukanlist.com`
   - Phase 2 → `dukanlist.com/register`
   - Phase 3 → `dukanlist.com/search?cat=doctor` (or relevant category)
8. **CTA button:** "Learn More" / "Sign Up" / "Get Quote" (match phase)
9. **URL parameters (UTM tracking):** Add this to the destination URL:
   ```
   ?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=phase1-awareness&utm_content=creative1
   ```
   This lets you track in Google Analytics or your own logs which ad drove which visitor.

### Step 5.4 — Approval & launch

1. Click **Review** → review every detail
2. Click **Publish**
3. Meta takes 4–24 hours to approve (usually <2 hours for non-political ads)
4. Once approved, ads start running automatically

### Step 5.5 — First 72 hours — monitoring

- Day 1: Just watch — Meta is learning your audience
- Day 2: Check CTR — should be >1.5% for region. If <0.5%, creative is weak.
- Day 3: Check CPM (cost per 1000 impressions) — should be ₹30–60 in rural North India
- Day 7: Look at "Audience insights" — see which age/gender clicks most. Refine.

## 6. Budget Plan (Starter — ₹300/day)

### Why ₹300/day?

In Mandi Dabwali, organic Instagram reach is limited (~3% of followers). With ₹300/day:
- ~10,000–15,000 impressions per day (estimated CPM ₹20–30)
- ~150–300 link clicks per day at 2% CTR
- Cost per click: ₹1–2 — extremely cheap for Tier-3 markets

### 60-day total budget breakdown

| Phase | Days | Daily | Total | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 — Awareness | 14 | ₹300 | ₹4,200 | 200k impressions, brand recall |
| Phase 2 — Owners | 21 | ₹500 | ₹10,500 | 200+ owner registrations |
| Phase 3 — Customers | 25 | ₹400 | ₹10,000 | 5,000+ website visits |
| **TOTAL 60 DAYS** | **60** | — | **₹24,700** | — |

### Where to spend the money

Within each phase, allocate:
- **70%** — Best-performing creative (the one with highest CTR after Day 3)
- **20%** — Second creative (different angle to avoid fatigue)
- **10%** — Experimental new creative (test new ideas weekly)

### When to pause / kill an ad

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| CTR < 0.5% after 5,000 impressions | Pause — creative is weak |
| CPM > ₹150 | Pause — audience targeting too narrow |
| 0 link clicks after ₹500 spent | Kill — destination broken or copy bad |
| CTR > 3% | Boost budget by 50% — winner |

## 8. What to Track (KPIs)

### Vanity metrics (look at, but don't optimise for)
- Likes
- Followers
- Comments

### Real metrics (these drive business)

| Metric | Goal (60 days) | How to measure |
|---|---|---|
| **Reach** | 80,000 unique people | Ads Manager → Reach column |
| **Link clicks** | 8,000 | Ads Manager → Link Clicks |
| **Website visits from Instagram** | 6,000 | Google Analytics → Acquisition → Instagram |
| **New owner registrations** | 200 | DB query: `SELECT COUNT(*) FROM businesses WHERE created_at > '2026-06-22' AND consent_method LIKE '%instagram%'` (if you add UTM tracking) |
| **Cost per registration** | < ₹50 | Phase 2 spend ÷ registrations |
| **Cost per website visit** | < ₹2 | Total spend ÷ link clicks |

### Weekly review checklist

Every Sunday evening, 30 minutes:
1. Open Ads Manager → last 7 days view
2. Note: Spend, Reach, CTR, Cost per Click
3. Compare to previous week
4. Identify best creative + worst creative
5. Adjust budget: shift ₹50/day from worst to best
6. Note any audience insights (age/gender shifts)
7. Plan next week's organic posts

## 9. Design Guidelines for Canva (or Designer)

### Brand colours
- **Primary saffron:** `#D97706`
- **Saffron deep:** `#92400E`
- **Cream:** `#FFFBEB`
- **Accent gold:** `#FBBF24`
- **Dark navy (for text contrast):** `#0F172A`

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Manrope or Plus Jakarta Sans, weight 800–900
- **Body:** Plus Jakarta Sans or Inter, weight 500–700
- **Hindi text:** Noto Sans Devanagari

### Visual principles
1. **One message per ad** — don't cram 3 features in one image
2. **Big text** — readable at 30% phone screen size (people scroll fast)
3. **Faces + photos** — beat illustrations 2x for engagement in our region
4. **Real shops** — generic stock photos perform worse than actual Dabwali shop photos
5. **Logo small, message big** — don't over-brand; the message is what stops the scroll

### Canva template suggestions (you can search these)
- "Indian small business Instagram"
- "Local directory app Instagram ad"
- "Saffron gradient Instagram post"

### Required dimensions
| Format | Size |
|---|---|
| Feed square | 1080 × 1080 |
| Story / Reel cover | 1080 × 1920 |
| Carousel slide | 1080 × 1080 (each) |
| Reel video | 1080 × 1920, 9:16, max 90s |

### Logo placement
Always include the DukanList 🥇 logo + URL `dukanlist.com` in a corner. Size: ~8–10% of total image area. Don't centre it — let the message dominate.

## 10. Common Pitfalls to Avoid

1. **❌ Pure Hindi (Devanagari) copy in ads** — feels formal, lower engagement. Hinglish in Roman script works 2x better in Dabwali.

2. **❌ Stock photos of generic Indian businesses** — visitors recognise them, feels fake. Use actual photos (with permission) of Dabwali shops.

3. **❌ Targeting too wide** — "All of Punjab" wastes budget. Start tight (10 km) and expand only if you're sure.

4. **❌ Optimising for "likes"** — likes don't pay bills. Optimise for link clicks or conversions.

5. **❌ Running same ad for 30 days** — ad fatigue kills performance after 2 weeks. Rotate creatives every 7–10 days.

6. **❌ Forgetting UTM parameters** — without UTMs, you can't tell which ad drove which result. Always tag URLs.

7. **❌ Ignoring comments** — Instagram rewards engaged accounts. Reply to every comment within 2 hours.

8. **❌ Posting at wrong times** — 3 AM posts get zero reach. Stick to 7:30 AM / 1:30 PM / 8:30 PM IST.

9. **❌ Promising what you don't deliver** — "Aapko har customer milega" sounds great but lies. "Apni dukaan online dikhayegi" is honest.

10. **❌ No follow-through on DM enquiries** — when an owner DMs asking about registration, reply within 30 min. Slow DMs kill conversion.
