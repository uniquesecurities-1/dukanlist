How this works:
  1. Your screenshot is drawn on a 1080 × 1920 canvas. The top 200 px becomes a saffron caption strip with the chosen English + Hindi text.
  2. If your screenshot is not 1080 wide, it auto-scales to fit while preserving aspect ratio.
  3. If your screenshot is taller than 1720 px (1920 − 200 strip), the top portion is used and the rest cropped. Pick screenshots that show the most important content near the top.
  4. Everything runs in your browser — no upload to any server.
