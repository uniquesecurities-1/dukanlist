**Use this as your copy-paste reference. Each section maps 1:1 to a Play Console field.**

Last updated: 26 Jun 2026
Publisher: DIGIMUTUAL GOALS PRIVATE LIMITED
Package: `com.dukanlist.twa`
AAB version: 1.0.0.0 (versionCode 1)

**Positioning principle:** DukanList is a utility — a clean local-business directory built for small Indian towns. The store listing leads with the product, not the founder. No mention of SEBI / AMFI registration, no cross-promotion of any other business, no claims like "trusted by 1000+ families". Only verifiable facts about the app itself.

## 0. Quick order of operations in Play Console

1. **Play Console → Create app** → fill basic details (Section 1 below)
2. **Set up your app** card → tick each item (Section 2-7 cover everything)
3. **App content** sidebar → fill Privacy Policy, Data Safety, Content Rating, Target audience, News app=No, COVID-19 contact tracing=No, Government app=No, Financial features=No, Ads=No
4. **Main store listing** → paste Section 3 + upload assets (Section 8 specs)
5. **Production → Create new release** → upload `DukanList.aab` → paste Section 9 release notes → Review → Submit
6. Estimated review time: **3-5 days** (established publisher account)
