## PROMPT 3 — 12-Second Vertical Video (Sora / Runway / Veo, 9:16)

A 12-second cinematic vertical video advertisement. 9:16 aspect ratio. 1080×1920 resolution. 24 fps. Apple-launch-grade production value. Editorial documentary style with subtle dynamism. No voiceover — visuals and minimalist music speak.

### Scene 1 (0–3 s) — The Send
Macro close-up of an Indian shopkeeper's hands: slight crow's-feet, a modest gold ring, holding a smartphone. Thumb taps a "Share on WhatsApp" button on a beautifully dark-themed digital business card UI — shop name visible on screen: **"MEHTA ELECTRONICS"**, gold 5-star rating, green Open Now pill, "Powered by DukanList" footer.
- Shallow depth of field. Warm key light. Subtle slow push-in.
- Sound: single soft haptic "tap" → gentle whoosh.

### Scene 2 (3–6 s) — The Receive
Quick cut to a young urban customer in a café — denim jacket, smartphone in hand. A WhatsApp notification slides down from the top: *"Mehta Electronics shared a contact card"*. She taps it. The vCard pop-up appears with a clean **"Save Contact"** button. She taps Save. A subtle gold checkmark pulses on screen.
- Bright UI confirmation chime.
- Background: ambient café bokeh, warm hanging lights.

### Scene 3 (6–9 s) — The Memory
Match-cut to the customer's phone contacts list scrolling — **"Mehta Electronics"** now appears with full details, profile photo, address, phone. Camera pulls back gently to show her smiling slightly, slipping the phone into her bag.
- Ambient music swells warmly.

### Scene 4 (9–12 s) — The Pride
Cut to a wide hero shot: confident Indian shopkeeper standing in front of his modern shop in golden-hour light, looking at his phone with quiet pride. Text overlay slides in from the bottom in premium Manrope ExtraBold font, one line at a time:

> **Your Card.**
> **Their Contacts.**
> **In One Tap.**

### End frame (last 1.5 s)
Centred DukanList logo (yellow mark + wordmark). Tagline below: *"Be the local business everyone remembers."* URL: **dukanlist.com**

**Color grade:** cinematic teal-and-orange, slight halation on highlights, fine film grain throughout.
**Audio:** minimalist piano + warm pad swell, single confirmation chime at Scene 2, smooth audio crossfade throughout. No voiceover.
**Mood:** aspirational, calm, Apple-launch confidence. Indian context, globally premium polish.

**Avoid:** shaky cam, gimmicky transitions, cartoon/comic typography, busy graphics, fake-looking AI faces, cluttered scenes, over-edited frames, dated zoom-in/zoom-out effects.

## Usage tips

- For ChatGPT image generation: paste the entire **PROMPT 1** or **PROMPT 2** block as one message. If the model truncates, drop the "Style references" and "Avoid" lines first.
- For Sora / Veo / Runway: paste **PROMPT 3** as-is. If the tool only takes ~500 chars, condense each scene to its first 2 sentences.
- After generation, run a second pass: "Same image, but make the phone screen UI sharper and the headline text more legible." This consistently improves readability.
- For WhatsApp Status export: ensure 1080×1920 px, ≤16 MB, MP4 H.264.
