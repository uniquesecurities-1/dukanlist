## 9. 🎨 Visual Style Guide

To keep the grid looking consistent (Apple/Mamaearth-grade brand feel):

**Colors — strict, no exceptions:**
- Orange `#FF6B1A` (CTA, accents, highlights)
- Navy `#1E3A8A` (text, dark backgrounds)
- White `#FFFFFF` (backgrounds)
- Off-white `#FAFAFA` (secondary backgrounds)
- Charcoal `#0F172A` (deep text)

**Fonts:**
- Headlines: **Manrope ExtraBold** or **Plus Jakarta Sans Bold**
- Body: **Inter Regular** / **Noto Sans Devanagari** for Hindi text
- Avoid: cursive fonts, Comic Sans, default Canva fonts

**Layout templates (build once in Canva, reuse forever):**
- **Shop spotlight:** Photo on top 60%, shop name in navy bold, USP in 1 line, rating + city pill below, logo bottom-right corner
- **Educational carousel:** Title on slide 1 with big number ("3 reasons..."), each subsequent slide = one tip with icon + 2 lines + footer logo
- **Story template:** Vertical 1080×1920, your logo top-left, brand color background, big title centered, CTA arrow bottom

**Logo placement rule:** Logo always in **bottom-right corner**, small enough to not distract (~8% of width), with 5% margin.
