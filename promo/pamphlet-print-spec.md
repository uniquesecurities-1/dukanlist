## 📄 Paper Recommendations (by budget)

| Tier | Paper Type | GSM | Approx Cost (Dabwali) | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Budget** | Maplitho (creamy white offset paper) | 90-100 GSM | ₹0.80–1.20 / copy | Door-to-door, newspaper insert, mass distribution (5000+ copies) |
| **Standard** ⭐ | Sunshine (bright white art paper) | 130 GSM | ₹1.50–2.00 / copy | **Recommended** — looks premium, holds color well, doesn't tear easily |
| **Premium** | Art paper matte finish | 170 GSM | ₹2.50–3.50 / copy | Hand-delivered to high-value prospects, partner meetings, exhibitions |
| **Luxury** | Art paper gloss + UV coating | 200 GSM | ₹4.00–6.00 / copy | Limited-edition VIP outreach — first 50 partners |

**My recommendation:** Start with **130 GSM Sunshine paper** — sweet spot of cost vs perceived quality. Doesn't look cheap, doesn't break the bank.

## 🎨 Print Process

### Option 1 — Digital print (small quantity)
- Up to 500 copies
- Cost: ₹1.50–2.50 / copy
- Turnaround: same-day or next-day
- Best for: testing, A/B variants, small batches

### Option 2 — Offset print (mass quantity) ⭐
- 1000+ copies
- Cost: ₹0.80–1.50 / copy (drops sharply at higher volumes)
- Turnaround: 2-3 days
- Best for: 2000+ copy run for Dabwali full coverage

**Pricing tip:** Negotiate hard at 2000+ copies. The base cost is mostly the plate-making (~₹500-800), so per-copy cost crashes after volume.

## 🎯 Distribution Strategy (after printing)

### Best methods for Mandi Dabwali

1. **Newspaper insert** (best ROI) — Punjab Kesari / Dainik Bhaskar local edition. Cost ~₹0.30/insert × 1000 copies = ₹300. Reaches 1000 homes overnight.
2. **Door-to-door in main bazaar** — Hire 2 students at ₹500/day, cover 200-300 shops in 2 days. Don't just slide under shutter — talk to shopkeeper, hand over personally, point to QR.
3. **Market stall presence** — Put a small table near Chotala Road or Old Grain Market on a Sunday morning. Hand out + sign people up live on a phone.
4. **Tea-shop / barbershop drop** — These have high foot traffic. Leave a stack of 10-20 with the owner (they'll let you in if you offer ₹50).
5. **WhatsApp followup** — When someone scans QR, they go to dukanlist.com/register. After they fill, send a thank-you WhatsApp the next day with your number.

### What to AVOID

- ❌ Don't dump 500 copies at one chowk — wind blows them away, looks like trash
- ❌ Don't print in 4-5 colors (only orange + navy + black). More colors = higher cost + cheap look
- ❌ Don't laminate — wastes money + people can't write on it
- ❌ Don't include a phone number that goes unanswered. If your number rings out, trust is destroyed.

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Print 2 variants, A/B test.** Print 500 of variant A (e.g. shopkeeper-focused hero) and 500 of variant B (e.g. category-focused hero). Distribute in different neighborhoods. Measure which gets more scans via the `?ref=` tracking. Print the winner at 5000 copies.

2. **Festival timing matters.** Distribute 1 week before a buying festival (Diwali, Lohri, Karva Chauth, Eid) — shopkeepers want online presence right before peak season.

3. **Bundle with a small freebie.** A 1-rupee sticker that says "DukanList Verified" or a tiny card with "Free WhatsApp tips for shopkeepers" — increases retention by 3-5x. People keep what has perceived value.

4. **Track the WhatsApp messages.** When you put your WhatsApp number on the pamphlet, set a saved auto-reply: *"Thanks for messaging! To list your shop on DukanList, visit https://dukanlist.com/register — takes 60 sec. For any help, just reply."*

5. **Reprint quickly.** If your first batch of 1000 gets 100+ registrations (10% conversion is excellent for pamphlets), reprint immediately for 5000 copies. Momentum decays fast.
