## 🎨 Universal Production Notes (apply to every brief below)

- **Aspect ratio:** square `1080×1080` for WhatsApp Status DP / Instagram feed; vertical `1080×1920` for Story / Status.
- **Brand colours (constrained palette — never use all at once):**
  - Ink Navy `#0F172A` — headlines, body
  - Cream `#FFFBF5` — backgrounds (NOT pure white)
  - Saffron `#FF6B1A` — single accent (CTA, divider, italic)
  - Editorial Gold `#D97706` — luxury accents (advocates, NGOs)
  - Deep Steel `#1E293B` — industrial / B2B
- **Typography baseline:**
  - Display: **Plus Jakarta Sans** (weight 800–900) — modern editorial
  - Body: **Manrope** (weight 500–600)
  - Devanagari: **Noto Sans Devanagari** (weight 700)
  - Serif (for advocates only): **Cinzel** or **Cormorant Garamond**
- **Layout rule of thirds:** Headline in upper third. Negative space in middle. CTA in bottom third. Never centred-vertical.
- **Always include in every prompt:**
  > *"Editorial campaign poster, shot like Apple/Lufthansa/Mercedes-Benz brand work. Restraint, breathing room, single hero focus. NO stock-flyer aesthetic, NO clutter, NO multiple text blocks, NO clip-art icons."*

A doctor's hands have spent 30 years healing — those same hands deserve a digital identity, not a "shop listing".

### 🏛 Reference Aesthetics


## 🎬 How to Iterate (Pro Tips)

1. **First pass:** Generate exactly as written. Don't tweak.
2. **If output is too busy** → Add to end of prompt:
   > *"REDUCE all visual elements. Single hero focus only. Triple the negative space. Remove any icons or badges."*
3. **If typography is wrong** → Add:
   > *"Render typography in pixel-perfect English (and Devanagari where specified). NO misspellings. Use Plus Jakarta Sans family. Maintain exact line-breaks as written."*
4. **If colors look off** → Specify:
   > *"Color grade strictly: ink-navy #0F172A, cream #FFFBF5, saffron #FF6B1A only. No other colors. No gradients."*
5. **If it looks "stock-photo-y"** → Add:
   > *"Editorial brand campaign aesthetic. Reference: [Apple / Mercedes / Lufthansa] brand book pages. NOT a Justdial / Sulekha / OLX flyer."*

## ⛔ The "Reject" Checklist

Before sending any generated poster on WhatsApp, ask:
- Does it look like a **stock-flyer template**? ❌ Regenerate.
- Are there **more than 3 emojis** on it? ❌ Regenerate.
- Is the text **more than 5 lines** total? ❌ Regenerate.
- Does it have **clip-art icons** (stethoscope/scales/calculator illustrations)? ❌ Regenerate.
- Does it look like it could be on the cover of **a top business magazine**? ✅ Send.
