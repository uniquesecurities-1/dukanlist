## 1️⃣1️⃣ LOGO USAGE DO's & DON'Ts

When using your logo in posters / reels / posts:

✅ **DO:**
- Always keep ≥5% clear space around the logo
- Use the SVG (vector) version for print/large posters
- Use the PNG version (transparent background) for overlays
- Logo can sit on white, off-white, or solid brand colors
- Minimum size: 24×24 pixels on screen (any smaller becomes illegible)

❌ **DON'T:**
- Stretch / distort proportions
- Place on busy photo backgrounds without a white safe-zone behind it
- Recolor the logo (orange + navy only)
- Add glow/shadow/3D effects to the logo itself
- Combine with another brand's logo without a clear partner ribbon
